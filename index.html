About Word of Life

Word of Life is the Bible app from Daily Word 360 — the whole Bible to read and listen to, with notes, favorite verses, and shareable verse cards.

Translation: the World English Bible (WEB) — a public-domain translation in modern English. The daily devotional posts use the NIV, but the NIV is copyrighted and cannot be included in a full Bible, so this app uses the WEB.

Listening: the “Listen” button reads aloud with your own device’s voice (Web Speech). It is not a recording — the voice and quality depend on your phone or computer.

Saving: your favorites and notes are saved on this device only (in this browser). They do not sync to another phone or computer — use the backup download in Notes to keep a copy.
