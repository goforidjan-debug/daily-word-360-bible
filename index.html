About Word of Life

Word of Life is the Bible app from Daily Word 360 — the whole Bible to read and listen to, with notes, favorite verses, and shareable verse cards.

Translation: Word of Life is in the WEB translation — the World English Bible, in simple English that’s easy to understand.

Features: listen to any chapter read aloud in your device’s voice, with calm music playing softly underneath. Save your favorite verses, write notes, and share beautiful verse cards. Your favorites and notes are saved on this device only.
